# TMP1 enclosure hardware

## Screws

Four thread-forming Torx pan screws from the **bottom**: through recessed heads in the bottom cover, through the PCBA `H1`–`H4` holes (3.5 mm), into pilots in the **top** bosses.

| Item | Choice |
|---|---|
| Type | Plastite / Plas-Fix 45 style, trilobular thread-forming for plastic |
| Size | M2.5 × 1.4 pitch × **8 mm** long |
| Head / drive | Pan, Torx |
| Example MPN | `TPXPM2.5X8D45` (zinc) or `TPXPM2.5X8BOD45` (black oxide) from MF Supply / equivalents |
| CAD stand-in | McMaster **90362A146** (ISO 14583 pan Torx M2.5×8) STEP in `references/` |
| Bottom | Ø5.6 × 2.2 mm counterbore, Ø3.0 mm through |
| Top boss pilot | 1.90 mm (about 80% thread engagement) |
| Boss / pad OD | 7.0 mm |

Confirm final pilot diameter on a PA12 test boss before locking a production screw lot. The McMaster STEP is machine-screw geometry for fit checks; order the Plastite for plastic bosses.

## LED light pipe

| Item | Choice |
|---|---|
| Part | Bivar **PLP1-6MM-F** (clear, **flat** face, rear panel press-fit) |
| Digi-Key | Search **PLP1-6MM-F** (flat T1 panel mount; typically 492-series) |
| Body length X | **6.0 mm** from flange seat toward the LED |
| Flange | Ø3.8 mm × 1.3 mm; **underside seats on ceiling underside** |
| Panel tip | 2.3 mm (0.090 in) through the top wall toward the outer face |
| Panel hole in top shell | Ø 3.10 mm (0.121 in) |
| Panel thickness | **2.3 mm** (matches tip for flush outer face; within PLP1 1.2–2.4 mm) |
| CAD | `references/bivar-PLP1-6MM-F.step` (datasheet-derived) |

Install from the inside of the top cover until the flange underside bottoms on the inner top face.
